Subject: Open Source Summit Europe 2026 – Trip Report

Hi all,

Last week I attended Open Source Summit Europe + Embedded Linux Conference + Zephyr Developer Summit,
held in Prague, October 7–9. Below is a summary of NXP's participation and the key themes from the
conference.


NXP PRESENCE

NXP had a strong showing across three tracks:
- Embedded Linux Conference (ELC) – 4 talks
- Zephyr Developer Summit – 3 talks
- PX4 Flight Control – 1 talk

NXP was also a Silver sponsor.


NXP TALKS

- The Upstream Way: Current State of i.MX SoCs in the Linux Kernel (Daniel Baluta) — Generated
  significant interest; many attendees confirmed active use of i.MX SoCs in their products.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaeH/the-upstream-way-current-state-of-imx-socs-in-the-linux-kernel-daniel-baluta-nxp-semiconductors

- Real-Time Performance Comparison of Linux, Zephyr and FreeRTOS (Zbynek Fedra & Mingkai Hu) —
  Benchmarked the three RTOSes side by side; practical data for product teams choosing a platform.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaaM/real-time-performance-comparison-of-linux-zephyr-and-freertos-zbynek-fedra-mingkai-hu-nxp

- Ten Years of Zephyr: Replace Linux, or Work With It? (Iuliana Prodan) — Examined where Zephyr fits
  alongside Linux in modern embedded systems; well-attended given Zephyr's 10th anniversary milestone
  at this conference.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaWm/ten-years-of-zephyr-replace-linux-or-work-with-it-iuliana-prodan-nxp-semiconductors

- Bringing IEC 60730 Class B Functional Safety to Zephyr (Andrej Butok) — Covered NXP's work on
  functional safety certification for Zephyr, relevant for industrial and appliance markets.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaaA/bringing-iec-60730-class-b-functional-safety-to-zephyr-andrej-butok-nxp-semiconductors

- Learning to Navigate: Challenges of RL with PX4 as a Real-Time Controller (Kyrillos Adeeb) — NXP's
  contribution to the PX4 track, covering reinforcement learning integration with PX4 on NXP hardware.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RadS/learning-to-navigate-challenges-of-rl-with-px4-as-a-real-time-controller-kyrillos-adeeb-nxp

- Optimizing AI for Resource-Constrained Edge Devices (Pavel Macenauer) — Techniques for deploying
  inference on memory- and power-limited hardware; directly applicable to i.MX NPU positioning.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2Rajk/optimizing-ai-for-resource-constrained-edge-devices-pavel-macenauer-nxp-semiconductors


HIGHLIGHT SESSIONS (OTHER SPEAKERS)

- Fireside Chat: Linus Torvalds & Dirk Hohndel — The traditional conference highlight. Linus on AI:
  "I think you need to be very careful using AI when doing something real and important," but also
  "I actually really like using AI — I use it to do things that I'm bad at." Measured, pragmatic view.

- Edge AI on Linux (Karim Yaghmour, Opersys) — Strong overview of the Edge AI landscape. Two
  approaches: AI built into SoC (NXP i.MX95, TI AM6xA, Qualcomm Dragonwing) vs. discrete AI
  accelerators (NXP Ara-2/Kinara, Hailo 10H, MemryX MX3, Axelera Metis). Sparked a question about
  NXP's plans for upstreaming the Neutron NPU driver.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2Rag4/edge-ai-on-linux-karim-yaghmour-opersys-inc

- Navigating the Complexity of Modern SoC Bootflows (Marco Felsch, Pengutronix) — Used i.MX6/8/9 as
  the running example to show how bootflow complexity has grown from the relatively straightforward
  i.MX6 to the multi-stage, multi-firmware flow on i.MX95.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2Rab5/navigating-the-complexity-of-modern-soc-bootflows-marco-felsch-pengutronix-ek

- Asymmetric Multi-Processing (AMP) for Embedded — Where Are We? (Wolfram Sang, Renesas) — Survey of
  the current AMP ecosystem in Linux; relevant to NXP's i.MX CM7 and remoteproc work.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaWd/asymmetric-multi-processing-amp-for-embedded-where-are-we-wolfram-sang-renesas-consultant

- A Clockwork Frequency: Bringing Spread Spectrum to the Linux Clock Subsystem (Dario Binacchi,
  Amarula) — EMC-driven clock spreading in the Linux clk framework; useful reference for i.MX clock
  subsystem work.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaYT/a-clockwork-frequency-bringing-spread-spectrum-to-the-linux-clock-subsystem-dario-binacchi-amarula-solutions

- Zephyr on the Big Core: Real-Time on the i.MX93 Cortex-A55 with Zephyr (Ryan Erickson, Ezurio) —
  Showed Zephyr running on an A-class core for deterministic workloads; directly relevant to NXP
  platforms.
  https://osselceu2026.sched.com/event/2RaiL/zephyr-on-the-big-core-real-time-on-the-imx93-cortex-a55-with-zephyr-ryan-erickson-ezurio


COMMUNITY & INDUSTRY FEEDBACK ON NXP UPSTREAMING

- Toradex acknowledged clear upstreaming progress in the i.MX tree over the past year. Key ask:
  improve the process for partner vendors submitting patches to the NXP BSP tree.

- Siemens stated directly that they want to use the Linux kernel mainline tree and are satisfied with
  upstreaming progress for i.MX8/9. They want NXP to continue at the same pace.

- General community feedback: strong demand for public access to the linux-nxp-rebase tree so partners
  can more easily upstream their own i.MX features.

- i.MX95 display upstreaming: Had a productive discussion with Marek Vasut and Frank Li on unblocking
  this. Action item: revive the mailing list thread with a short status summary and clear next steps.

- Overall sentiment: NXP's presence was well received. Multiple attendees asked for broader NXP
  representation, specifically from display/camera and NPU subsystems, at future events.


KEY TAKEAWAYS

1. AI in open source is mainstream, but with caution. The dominant theme across the conference. Most
   contributors now use AI tools; maintainers expect AI-flagged review comments (sashiko and similar
   tools) to be addressed before a human reviewer will look at a patch.

2. NXP upstreaming credibility is growing. Feedback from Toradex, Siemens, and independent developers
   was consistently positive. The work is visible and valued; continued investment is expected.

3. Edge AI is a competitive landscape. NXP i.MX95 is visible as a first-class example of integrated
   AI silicon. The Neutron NPU upstream story needs to be defined and communicated proactively.

4. Zephyr's 10th anniversary signals maturity. The project is moving from early adoption to production
   use in safety-critical and automotive segments. NXP's functional safety and real-time work is well
   positioned.

5. Action required — i.MX95 display: Resume mailing list discussion with Marek Vasut / Frank Li;
   provide summary and concrete next steps to unblock upstream.

6. Action required — Partner access to linux-nxp-rebase: The community wants it. Align internally on
   the policy and timeline.
